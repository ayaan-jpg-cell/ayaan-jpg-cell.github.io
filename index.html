Services

What I do

Most attacks on small businesses start with an email, a weak password, or a fake payment request. These services close those gaps first.

Security Checkup

A walk-through of your email accounts, passwords, backups, Wi-Fi, computers, and who has access to what. You get a short report that ranks the fixes that matter most.

Based on CISA Cyber Essentials and NIST CSF 2.0

Setup and fixes

I turn on multi-factor sign-in, set up backups and a password manager, and tighten your Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace settings. You get written notes on everything I changed.

Microsoft 365 · Google Workspace · Routers and Wi-Fi

Staff scam training

A 45-minute session on phishing emails, fake invoices, gift card requests, and payment scams, built around the tricks that target small offices.

In person or online · Up to 25 staff

Safe AI setup

Using ChatGPT or similar tools at work? I help you set rules for what data goes in, choose business accounts with the right privacy settings, and train staff to use them safely.

AI use policy · Account setup · Staff guide
